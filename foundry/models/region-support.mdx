# Model Region Support

Model availability varies by Azure region. Check this guide to plan your deployments.

## Key Regions

### Americas
- **East US**: Most models available
- **East US 2**: Most models available
- **West US**: Limited models
- **Canada East**: Most models available

### Europe
- **West Europe**: Most models available
- **North Europe**: Most models available
- **France Central**: Selected models
- **Switzerland North**: Selected models

### Asia Pacific
- **Southeast Asia**: Most models available
- **Australia East**: Most models available
- **Japan East**: Most models available

## Model Availability

### GPT-4o
- East US, West Europe, Sweden Central
- Global Standard SKU available

### GPT-4
- East US, West Europe, France Central
- Standard and Provisioned

### GPT-3.5-turbo
- Available in most regions
- Lowest cost option

### Foundry Direct Models
- Limited regional availability
- Check model card for specifics

## Checking Availability

Use Azure Portal to check real-time availability:

1. Navigate to Foundry portal
2. Open Model Catalog
3. Select model
4. View **Regional Availability** tab

See [Model Deployment](/foundry/models/deployment) for deployment instructions.
